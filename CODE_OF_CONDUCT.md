# Community code of conduct

Storm Harbor participants are expected to contribute respectfully and make the project welcoming regardless of background, identity, experience or organizational affiliation.

Use constructive, technical criticism; respect privacy and consent; give credit; and resolve disagreements through evidence and the documented review process. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, disclosure of private information and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to a [project maintainer](MAINTAINERS.md) using an available private contact channel. Do not publish personal details in a public report. A maintainer with a conflict of interest must not decide their own report; an independent reviewer must be designated before a decision.

Maintainers may ask for a correction, remove harmful content or restrict participation in proportion to the incident. Reports and responses should minimize disclosure and retaliation is prohibited. This policy applies to project repositories, discussions and project-managed community spaces.
